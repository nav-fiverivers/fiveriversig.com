Capabilities

Five stages.
One accountable team.

Every project passes through the same five stages. The difference at Five Rivers is that the same company owns all five, so nothing gets lost in a handoff.

Stage 01

Land & Development

We help owners find the right ground before a single line is drawn. As a licensed brokerage, Five Rivers sources sites, reads zoning and utilities, runs feasibility, and structures the deal so that what gets bought can actually get built.

  • Site selection and acquisition
  • Licensed brokerage (DRE No. 02060161, 01460685)
  • Feasibility and due diligence
  • Zoning, utility, and access review
  • Pro forma and budget support
  • Deal structuring

Stage 02

Design & Entitlement

Our design division produces complete construction documents in-house, the same documents our crews build from. Then we carry the project through zoning, plan check, and permitting ourselves. Constructability is engineered in from the first sketch, and approvals are managed by people who know what the field needs.

  • Conceptual and schematic design
  • Full construction documents
  • Structural and MEP coordination
  • Entitlement and land use
  • Permitting and agency coordination
  • Entitlement consulting for outside owners
Drawn
in-house.
Built
in-house.

Stage 03

Financing

A project with a plan and no lender is still an idea. We procure lenders, package the loan, and line up the capital stack alongside design, so financing closes when the permits do and construction starts without a gap.

  • Lender procurement
  • Construction and permanent loan packaging
  • Budget and schedule exhibits lenders expect
  • Draw schedule coordination
Lender-
ready
from
day one.

Stage 04

Construction

This is where the self-performance model pays off. Framing, concrete, electrical, plumbing, and fueling systems are performed by Five Rivers crews under one superintendent. No waiting on a sub's calendar, no finger-pointing between trades, one standard of quality on every site.

  • Ground-up commercial and industrial
  • Structural framing
  • Concrete and foundations
  • Electrical systems
  • Plumbing and MEP
  • Fueling systems, canopies, tanks, and retanks
  • Tenant improvements and remodels
  • Project management and scheduling

Stage 05

Delivery & Operations

We build for the person who has to run the building. Because we understand the mechanical systems we install, every decision is made with uptime in mind: fewer shutdowns, lower lifetime operating cost, and a turnover package the operator can actually use.

  • Commissioning and startup
  • Operator training and turnover documents
  • Maintenance-first system design
  • Service calls and ongoing support
