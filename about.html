About

Built on
self-performance.

Five Rivers was founded on a simple premise: the fastest, most reliable builders are the ones who control their own work. So we design it, permit it, finance it, and build it ourselves.

Our story

California built.
Bakersfield based.

Five Rivers Investment Group & Construction is a licensed California general contractor and developer headquartered in Bakersfield, working across the Central Valley and the state.

We grew up in fueling and travel centers, where the schedule is contractual and the systems underground have to work for twenty years. That work taught us to keep the critical trades in-house, to draw the plans we build from, and to treat approvals and financing as part of the job rather than someone else's problem.

Today the same model carries retail, restaurant, industrial, medical, and multifamily projects for owners who come back project after project. We are a growing, founder-led company, and we hire people who hold themselves to the same standard.

How we work

Four rules
on every site.

Precision over speed

Aggressive timelines are the norm here, but we hit them by sequencing well, not by cutting corners.

Control the critical path

If a trade can stop the schedule, we perform it ourselves. No excuses borrowed from a subcontractor.

Build for the operator

Every mechanical decision is made for uptime and maintenance, because the building has to work long after we leave.

Say it, then do it

Budgets and dates are commitments. When something changes, you hear it from us first, with a plan.

Leadership

Founder-led.
Field-tested.

Jessie Brar

Founder & Principal

Jessie founded Five Rivers and leads its development side: finding the ground, structuring the deal, and carrying projects through city approvals. A licensed California real estate broker (DRE No. 01460685), Jessie has led the firm's land and entitlement work on projects such as The Gateway at Delano, a Starbucks-anchored retail center at Highway 99 and County Line Road, and its fueling and retail developments across the Central Valley.

Nav Brar

Director
